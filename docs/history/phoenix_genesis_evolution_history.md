# Phoenix Genesis Evolution History

## Project Identity

Phoenix Genesis is not designed as a simple trading bot.

The original architectural vision was to build a market intelligence platform capable of:

- Understanding market conditions
- Reasoning before action
- Learning from decisions
- Improving strategies through experience
- Evolving its own intelligence layers over time


Core philosophy:

Analysis First.
Decision Before Execution.
Learning From Outcome.
Evolution Through Memory.
